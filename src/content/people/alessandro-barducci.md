Alessandro Barducci received the B.Sc. degree in Mechanical Engineering in 2023 and the M.Sc. degree in Mechanical Engineering in 2026, both from Politecnico di Milano, Italy. He is currently a Ph.D. student at the University of Pavia, Pavia, Italy, and a member of RAMSLAB.

His research interests include robotic composite manufacturing, automated fiber deposition, robotic systems integration, and advanced control strategies for manufacturing processes.

He has contributed to the design and development of a hybrid fiber deposition head combining filament winding and automated fiber placement techniques, with particular emphasis on material feeding and tension control.
